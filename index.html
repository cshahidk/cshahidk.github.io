About Me

Researcher, educator, and principal investigator in computer vision and AI.

I am an Associate Professor at Xi'an Eurasia University and a former Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the Research & Development Institute of Northwestern Polytechnical University in Shenzhen, China. As a Principal Investigator (PI), I lead funded research projects in computer vision, multidimensional imaging, and artificial intelligence, and have contributed to multiple National Natural Science Foundation of China (NSFC) projects.

My research spans interrelated sub-areas of machine learning and computer vision, with a unifying theme of constructing efficient and robust techniques using self-organized autonomous entities in robotics and artificial intelligence.

I received my PhD in Information and Communication Engineering from the Harbin Institute of Technology (2019), an MS in Electronics Science and Technology from Xi'an Jiaotong University (2015), and a BEE in Electronics Engineering from COMSATS University (2010).

My near-term research focuses on deep learning and computer vision, image fusion and feature recognition, multidimensional imaging systems, and robotics and artificial intelligence. I have authored/co-authored numerous SCI/EI-indexed journal and conference papers and hold licensed patents in electro-optical sensing and target classification.
